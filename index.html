I'm a Computer Science Honours graduate from the University of Manitoba with a passion for building backend systems that are clean, reliable, and production-ready. I've spent the last two years shipping real code across fintech, travel, and education.

My sweet spot is data engineering and backend APIs — whether that's designing ingestion pipelines in Python, migrating legacy Oracle jobs to Go, or wiring up GraphQL mutations with Rails. I care deeply about correctness, traceability, and systems that hold up under load.

Currently at IGM Financial, building data infrastructure that powers real financial decisions.
